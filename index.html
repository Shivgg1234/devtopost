Privacy Model & Architecture

Acoustic Atlas is built with strict privacy-by-design principles:

  • Zero Audio Storage: Microphone audio is resampled in RAM into ~0.96s windows, classified by YAMNet, and discarded immediately. No raw PCM audio or recording is ever written to disk or sent anywhere.
  • 100% On-Device Inference: Machine Learning classification happens entirely inside your browser using WebAudio and TensorFlow.js.
  • Offline-First & Local Storage: All scores, spot names, and timestamps remain exclusively inside your browser's IndexedDB. No server accounts, tracking, or network calls are required.

How Scoring Works

Every 0.96-second audio window is evaluated across 521 AudioSet classes configured in config/classes.json:

$$\text{Nature Score} = \frac{\text{mean}(P_{\text{nature}})}{\text{mean}(P_{\text{nature}}) + \text{mean}(P_{\text{human}})} \times 100$$

Silence ($RMS < 0.005$) and microphone wind noise are automatically detected and flagged to prevent misleading score skews.
